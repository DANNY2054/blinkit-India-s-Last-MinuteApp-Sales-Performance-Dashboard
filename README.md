# blinkit-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
 An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing $1.20M in Blinkit grocery sales — uncovering 7 key business insights across outlet types, locations, and product categories using DAX &amp; Power Query.

I wanted to understand what actually drives sales in a grocery delivery app like Blinkit. So I took real sales data, cleaned it up, and built a dashboard that answers real business questions — not just pretty charts.
Anyone can open this dashboard and understand the business in under 5 minutes.

📊 The Numbers
💰 Total Sales       →  $1.20M
📈 Average Sale      →  $141
⭐ Customer Rating   →  3.97 / 5
📦 Transactions      →  8,523

💡 What I Discovered
Healthy products are winning
65% of all revenue comes from Low Fat products. Customers are clearly shifting toward healthier choices.
Small cities beat big cities
Tier 3 cities generate $472K — more than metro Tier 1 cities at $336K. Nobody expected that.
Fruits & Vegetables lead sales
Top selling category at $178K, followed by Snack Foods at $175K.
Older stores outperform newer ones
Outlets from 1998 still generate $205K — 57% above average. Trust built over decades shows in the numbers.
Medium outlets are the sweet spot
Medium sized stores generate 42% of all revenue — more than large or small outlets.
